Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Minewdjhu Studios ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Stream Shorts Pipeline application ("the Service"), including its integration with TikTok's API.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your TikTok account to the Service through TikTok Login Kit, we access the following information, limited to the scopes you explicitly authorize:

  • Basic profile info (user.info.basic) — your TikTok open ID, display name, and avatar, used to identify your connected account within the Service.
  • Video publishing permissions (video.publish, video.upload) — used only to upload and post video content to your TikTok account on your behalf, and only when you initiate an upload through the Service.

We do not collect passwords, payment information, or private messages. We do not access any TikTok data outside the scopes listed above.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected is used solely to operate the Service: to identify your authorized account, and to upload and publish the video content you choose to submit through the pipeline. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Access tokens and identifiers required to operate the Service are stored only for as long as your account remains connected. You may revoke the Service's access at any time via your TikTok account settings, after which we delete any stored tokens associated with your account.

4. Third-Party Services

The Service integrates with TikTok's official API to provide its functionality. Your use of TikTok itself is governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, which we do not control.

5. Your Rights

You may disconnect the Service from your TikTok account at any time. You may also request deletion of any data we hold about you by contacting us using the information below.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

7. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to contact@example.com — update this with your real contact address.
