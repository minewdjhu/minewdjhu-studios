The Story So Far

From Jackumentaries to Minewdjhu

Minewdjhu's content has always leaned on personality over polish - the same reactive, self-deprecating energy and a chat that gets treated like part of the cast, whatever game happens to be on that day. Here's how it started.

Age 7

The first camera

Jack starts filming on an old camera under the name minewdjh, calling the videos Jackumentaries.

06 Aug 2018

Minewdjhu is born

After losing his old Roblox account, Jack rebuilds under a new name - and this time it sticks. Each part of it means something:

MINE Minecraft
W Win
DJ Disc jockey
JH Jack Henderson
U Ultimate

MINE was Minecraft, Jack's favourite game at the time - he sank hours into a world called H&H, which he later revisited on stream. W was win, for a belief that everyone's good at their own thing. DJ stands for disc jockey, the job Jack dreamed of doing when he was younger. JH is simply his initials. And U was added at the end on his mum's suggestion.

"I remember when I was upset about losing my account and my mum helped me create another. She decided I could put U at the end of my name, which would stand for Ultimate." - Jack, on choosing the name
2019

Spreading the word

The Company Behind It

Minewdjhu Studios

Minewdjhu Studios is the parent company behind Minewdjhu. It holds the rights and likeness to the name, and runs the tools, projects, and small team that keep the channel moving day to day.

The Studio also keeps an informal, non-paid partnership with SoundSightUK, a separate Audio Visual company also run by Jack.

Projects & Builds

What I'm building

Viewer experience is at the heart of what I do. I can have up to ten projects in development at once because I want Minewdjhu to be more than somewhere to watch a stream: I want it to be a place where people can take part, contribute, and feel involved in a community. These projects support that goal, alongside my experiments in software and robotics.

Video Automation

Stream Shorts Automation

Finds moments from streams, builds each clip around the stream's personality and context, adds captions, and prepares Shorts for YouTube and TikTok. The priority is making clips feel like Minewdjhu, not generic AI content.

Video Automation

StreamVODAutomator: Finding The Stream Within The Stream

Finds the strongest 10 to 40 minutes of a stream using conversation, chat, audio, and discussion, then turns that segment into a titled, described YouTube video. It asks: if someone only watched one part of this stream, what should it be?

Stream Intelligence

Stream Radar: Stream Production Intelligence

Tracks games, tournaments, creator events, updates, and other streaming opportunities, syncing information between my computer and laptop through a cloud system connected to my server. It is designed to help me spot moments worth streaming.

Creator Tools

MWD Creator Command Center

A personal operating system for Minewdjhu that brings projects, streams, goals, tasks, dates, opportunities, content planning, and external events together. Stream Radar is integrated so important opportunities can be surfaced without relying on memory alone.

Operating System

MWD OS: An Operating System Nobody Asked For

An operating system concept intended to run on its own hardware, with a desktop, apps, games, MWD services, and deliberately unnecessary Minewdjhu features. It is an experiment in seeing how far a question like “I wonder if I could build that” can go.

Studio Production

Something BIG Is Coming Next Year

A new story project set three years after the original incident, with details being kept under wraps for now. It is planned to be hosted mainly under Minewdjhu Studios and developed in partnership with SoundSightUK, with time taken to get it right.

Community Game

The Minewdjhu Economy Stock Market Game

A fictional Minewdjhu economy with MWD and player-created coins, profiles, leaderboards, achievements, events, changing stock charts, arcades, and casino-style games. It uses usernames and numeric PIN login rather than passwords, does not use real money, and is designed to give the community activities, events, a shop, and reasons to follow project launches.

Game Development

MWD 6: The Best GTA Spin-off Ever Made

A deliberately ridiculous Minewdjhu-made GTA-style game with an explorable world, vehicles, missions, characters, police, radio, comedy, and a fully voice-acted cast. Even the sound effects are intended to be made by my own voice, from engines to “pew pew pew”.

Robotics

Vessel Bot: Yana's Remote Attendance Robot

A mobile telepresence robot designed to let Yana remotely attend visits to Scotland, move around on specialised tracks, see through a 360-degree VR camera connected to a Meta Quest setup, and communicate with people. Its larger wooden body houses three speakers, with conduit helping keep the wiring secure.

Robotics & AI

Project MWD 1.0: A Humanoid Learning Robot

A roughly six-foot humanoid robot based on my Roblox avatar, Mr Robot, with working limbs and fingers. The long-term goal is for it to learn from experience, walk, interact, remember people and context, navigate, and communicate naturally using a broad collection of sensors. The ultimate goal is to make it dance.

MinewdSMP History

Interactive Lore & Saga Archive

MinewdSMP is Jack's privately owned Minecraft server, which he runs, updates, and optimises himself. Its history brings together two earlier Realms into one unified world on a server, with the first Realm dating back nearly three years as of 2026. The world has developed its own shared history through community events, disputes, ceremonies, and ongoing storylines.

Court Cases

Community Courtroom

MinewdSMP court cases turn player disputes into live, roleplay-driven events. A judge oversees proceedings while a prosecutor presents evidence, the defence responds, witnesses can be heard, and community members can serve as a jury. Screenshots and other records help build cases, and in some trials jurors vote using physical in-game items.

The Case of Star: Star was accused of griefing multiple bases, including William's island, and stealing Shulker boxes containing other players' armour. With Star absent, Charlie presented evidence as prosecutor. The jury voted unanimously guilty, and Star was banned.

The Case of Charlie: The first MinewdSMP court case to feature a practising lawyer, Nic, involved Charlie, who faced accusations involving the death of his horse Dinnerbone, alleged griefing, and restricted items such as bedrock. Prosecutor Yana argued the case, while Charlie and defence lawyer Nic, a practising lawyer, raised the “John Wick clause” and disputed how the rules applied. The outcome was a negotiated one-week banishment from his current zone, requiring him to relocate and establish a new settlement.

Sagas

Server Conflicts & Memorials

Some incidents become longer-running sagas, while others bring the community together for a shared event. Crystal became known for repeated griefing and attempts to return on alternate accounts. No formal conviction took place because repeated incidents, ban evasion, and further damage made immediate moderation more practical than waiting for a trial. The saga remains one part of a much wider community story.

Horsinho the 2nd's funeral: A funeral was held for a horse who had been with the server owner from the beginning. Jack built the church, grave, and portal in four days, adjusting the design to keep it simple and in line with the owner's wishes. Guests arrived in suits and tuxedos, Yana delivered a speech remembering the horse's bravery and love of flowers and open scenery, and attendees placed flowers around the grave or into the coffin. The ceremony mixed sincere remembrance with the server's usual chaotic humour.
