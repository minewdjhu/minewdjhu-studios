Minewdjhu started with an old camera when Jack was seven. It's grown into a stream, a studio, and a handful of people trying to do it properly.

Minewdjhu Studios is the company built around it — the rights, the tools, and the team that keeps it running. This page covers both the story and the studio.

The Story So Far

From Jackumentaries to Minewdjhu

Minewdjhu's content has always leaned on personality over polish — the same reactive, self-deprecating energy and a chat that gets treated like part of the cast, whatever game happens to be on that day. Here's how it started.

Age 7

The first camera

Jack starts filming on an old camera under the name minewdjh, calling the videos Jackumentaries.

06 Aug 2018

Minewdjhu is born

After losing his old Roblox account, Jack rebuilds under a new name — and this time it sticks. Each part of it means something:

MINE Minecraft
W Win
DJ Dream job
JH Jack Henderson
U Ultimate

MINE was Minecraft, Jack's favourite game at the time — he sank hours into a world called H&H, which he later revisited on stream. W was win, for a belief that everyone's good at their own thing. DJ was Jack's first dream job, before the channel took over. JH is simply his initials. And U was added at the end on his mum's suggestion.

"I remember when I was upset about losing my account and my mum helped me create another. She decided I could put U at the end of my name, which would stand for Ultimate." — Jack, on choosing the name
2019

Spreading the word

Always determined to show off his channel in any way possible, Jack designed, wore, and sold custom merch — even proudly wearing it to his last day of primary school.

14 Jul 2020

First video

Fortnite Gameplay: Episode 1 goes up during the first COVID lockdown, while Jack's in Year 6.

9 Apr 2026

Back on stream

Jack returns with Hang Out & Chat, a fresh start after a hard few months, and decides to put the work in properly this time.

Three months later

Twitch Affiliate

Jack becomes a paid Twitch affiliate partner — three months of consistent streaming, not a lucky break.

The Company Behind It

Minewdjhu Studios

Minewdjhu Studios is the parent company behind Minewdjhu. It holds the rights and likeness to the name, and runs the tools, projects, and small team that keep the channel moving day to day.

The Studio also keeps an informal, non-paid partnership with SoundSightUK, a separate Audio Visual company also run by Jack.

Projects & Builds

What the studio's put together

Jack takes pride in using his electronic technological knowledge to build things beneficial to the channel. He works tirelessly and independently to ensure everything he creates is perfect to develop these .

Gate A

Telepresence Robot

A home-built telepresence robot that let a moderator based in Jordan attend a meeting in Scotland remotely — one of the studio's stranger side projects, and one of its favourites.

Gate B

Stream Shorts Pipeline

An automated tool that manages and uploads video content straight to TikTok, built on TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Gate C

Automated VOD Pipeline

A companion tool that edits and compiles VOD footage into high-quality shorts, feeding the raw material the Shorts Pipeline then publishes.

Technical Backbone

Studios & Infrastructure

Minewdjhu Studios isn't just a brand name; it operates out of a real workshop combining hardware engineering, custom audio design, and custom-coded broadcast pipelines.

Workshop

The Hardware & Workshop

Deeply tied to Jack's background with SoundSight UK and hands-on electronics, the studio features a dedicated workshop. This space handles the fabrication of custom box speakers built out of unorthodox containers, high-end audio equipment repairs, and heavy fabrication like the custom electric go-kart project.

Automation

The Stream Architecture

Behind every broadcast is a custom technical pipeline. Utilizing tools like Social Stream Ninja and custom Discord bot automations, the studio bridges raw community interactions with stream overlays to keep broadcasts seamless and interactive.

Creator Philosophy

About Jack & The Method

"Streams aren't supposed to be sterile infomercials; they're unscripted, highly flawed hangouts where half the fun is laughing through the tech failures live with chat." — Jack Henderson

Built on radical honesty and absolute transparency, the studio embraces unpolished, personality-first content where viewers are treated less like an audience and more like part of the crew.
